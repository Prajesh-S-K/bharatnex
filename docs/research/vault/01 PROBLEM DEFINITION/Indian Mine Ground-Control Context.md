# Indian Mine Ground-Control Context

> [!success] Verified evidence currently available
> DGMS Technical Circular 02 of 2020 provides an official Indian basis for systematic slope monitoring in opencast coal and metalliferous mines.

## Source-supported findings

- The circular says a suitable slope-monitoring system should be customized to local needs to support timely withdrawal from an area likely to be affected by impending slope failure (CLM-0002; SRC-0002).
- It connects planning of mechanized opencast workings—including ultimate pit slope, dump slope, and monitoring—to a scientific study under the provision it cites (CLM-0003; SRC-0002).
- Therefore, Geo-Sentry cannot defend a universal sensor layout or alarm threshold. Mine-specific scientific study and competent professional judgement remain necessary. This final statement is an engineering inference from SRC-0002, not a quoted rule.

## What is not established

- That Geo-Sentry satisfies DGMS requirements
- That any current prototype is approved for mine deployment
- That one monitoring technology or threshold is suitable for all Indian mines
- That underground or hazardous-area deployment is covered by this opencast circular

## Jury verdict

**PARTIAL / NOT JURY-READY.** The regulatory need is supported, but current statutes, mine-type applicability, approvals, certification, and qualified expert interpretation remain open.
