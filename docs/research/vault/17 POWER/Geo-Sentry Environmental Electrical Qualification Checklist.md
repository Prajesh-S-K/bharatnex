# Geo-Sentry Environmental Electrical Qualification Checklist

## Environment definition

- [ ] Mine type and hazardous-area classification documented by competent authority
- [ ] Ambient min/max, solar loading, rain, humidity, dust, corrosion, altitude and vibration defined
- [ ] Internal enclosure temperature measured under worst credible conditions

## Power evidence

- [ ] Real load profile measured, including sensor warm-up, radio TX/retries and faults
- [ ] Energy per cycle and daily energy reported with uncertainty
- [ ] Battery exact model, lot traceability, BMS and datasheet revision recorded
- [ ] Charge/discharge inhibit tested at temperature boundaries
- [ ] Solar yield, shade and autonomy tested using site conditions
- [ ] Brownout, reverse polarity, surge, short circuit and recovery tested

## Component derating

- [ ] Every active/passive component checked against internal temperature, voltage and power
- [ ] Resistor tolerance/TCR/self-heating included in sensor error budget
- [ ] Capacitor voltage, dielectric, temperature and lifetime reviewed
- [ ] Connectors/cable glands checked for current, ingress, corrosion and service cycles
- [ ] PCB material, coating and contamination risks reviewed

## Qualification boundary

- [ ] Laboratory evidence is not presented as mine qualification
- [ ] Open-cast build is not presented as underground hazardous equipment
- [ ] Required BIS/DGMS/IEC route is documented using current sources
- [ ] Exact certified configuration and approval identifiers are preserved

## Verdict

**NOT PASSED.** No environmental qualification dataset has been registered.
