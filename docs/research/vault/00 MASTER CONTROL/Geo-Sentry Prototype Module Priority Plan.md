## Non-negotiable boundary

The deterministic safety path must continue to operate when ML, the local LLM, RAG, the agent, or internet access is unavailable. The agent may investigate, explain, draft, and recommend within permission boundaries; it may not rewrite raw measurements, suppress critical alerts, or change safety thresholds.
